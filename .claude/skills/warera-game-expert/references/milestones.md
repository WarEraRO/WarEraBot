# Milestones

Permanent (lifetime) ladders, one per activity; progress never resets `[RULE]`. They advance from the same actions as
missions.

## Ladders (15) `[RULE]`

Donate money to MU/country · Deal damage · Participate in battles · Earn XP total · Contribute to resistance · Craft
items · Obtain battlefield loot items · Open cases · Help MU members · Dismantle items · Hit in battles · Obtain cases from
battle loot · Cross regions on the map · Collect wooden cases · Eat food.

## Tiers and XP

| Tier | XP on claim |
|---|---|
| 6 | 60 |
| 7 | 70 |
| 9 | 90 |
| 10+ | 100 (cap) |

`[RULE~]` The pattern suggests **min(10 × tier, 100)**, but tiers 1–5 and 8 are not stated `[INFER]`. Targets grow fast
(high tiers need hundreds of thousands of hits, millions of damage, thousands of crafts) — exact targets are `[GAP]`.

## Claiming `[RULE]`

- Manual. If one action crosses several tiers, all become claimable and pay out in one claim.
- `[OBS]` Unclaimed Milestone XP can be banked to jump levels right before elections (vote/candidacy thresholds).

## Rarity `[RULE]`

Each tier shows the share of **active** players who reached it or higher, recomputed once per day (same as badge rarity;
badge tooltips show % and count). Informational only.

## Leaderboards

"Missions collected" and "Milestone Tiers" rank long-term activity, not level.
