# Strategy guidelines and checklists

Everything here is **`[STRAT-RO]` / `[POLICY-RO]`** — Romanian community recommendations and procedures. Present them as
advice or internal policy, never as game rules.

## Executive summary (guide)

1. Join Discord and verify (roles, live orders, help with skill distribution, NAPs, bounties, contracts, jobs, priorities).
2. Build a sustainable economy first: economy points until ~level 15, develop companies gradually, watch bonuses, keep liquidity.
3. Don't respec without a plan: optimizer, required gear, 7–14 days of consumables, ~500 money reserve.
4. On Fight: Pill daily, fight only coordinated; target ~0.15–0.20 money/1k damage (incl. cases/loot, excl. bounties); follow
   red/priority orders.
5. `[VERSIONED]` Until v0.27.4: think in regions — you fought from where you stood and every move cost Stamina or oil. Since
   v0.28 (2026-11-03) you hit from anywhere; pick battles by orders and bonuses only.
6. Recycle fight value: scrap, cases (incl. wooden), loot and company output extend the fight.
7. Support common infrastructure: steady donations to country and MU fund paper, oil, region construction, orders, bounties, contracts.

## Checklists

**New player**: chose Romania at sign-up · verified on Discord, joined #ro-newbie-chat · email login on, codes never shared ·
daily reward claimed, 7-day calendar understood · beginner missions (party, vote, MU, profile picture, new company), claimed
manually · points per the economy plan to level 15 · second company built, heading to 6 companies with AE 5 · sell blue+ gear,
use knife + max-stat green gear · don't craft Common/Uncommon/Rare · know residence on the map, don't waste oil · joined an MU.

**Daily — Economy**: claimed daily reward, missions, set rewards, Milestone tiers · rerolled expensive missions · Energy at the best
after-tax job · Entrepreneurship self-work · storage emptied before full · balance enough for wages · region bonuses/deposits
changed? · wooden case within Stamina (until ~2026-11-10)? · market opportunity below accepted value? · sustainable donation possible?

**Daily — Economy from v0.27** (2026-10-26, `[RULE]`): claim rewards and missions · self-work with Energy · money for the hourly
payroll · storage not full (it costs happiness) · one training draw per employee while Potential lasts · answer employee events
before 36 h · check new candidates (00:00 UTC) when a Break Room slot is free · watch happiness against the tax rate (quit below 30)
· log in at least every 3 days so events keep coming.

**Before a Fight respec**: see [skills-and-respec.md](skills-and-respec.md).

**Before a Pill**: see [ammunition-food-pills.md](ammunition-food-pills.md).

**Choosing/verifying a build**: daily cost vs income and 7–14-day budget · cost per 1k, not total damage · damage per money (equipment
valuation) and armor/dodge breakpoints before expensive pieces · resources for ≥7 days (Pills, food, ammo, spares, oil) · return to
economy planned (7-day cooldown, which companies reactivate).

**MU commander**: see [military-units.md](military-units.md).

**Government during a fight**: priorities ordered (#1, #2…) with updated descriptions incl. location · NAP/alliance/pact/Main Enemy
checked, alliance share of world development known · bunker/base/pacification and supply lines checked · treasury has paper, oil,
bounty/contract budget · bounty announced before activation, not cannibalizing a contract · MUs aligned, no duplicated costs ·
hourly coverage and someone able to modify/cancel orders · citizens updated after each major front change.

**Candidates and leadership handover**: availability (daily, critical hours) · access to government channels, bot, documents ·
knowledge of treasury, paper/oil, NAPs, alliance, proxies · list of ongoing commitments, contracts and active orders handed over ·
change announced with contacts.

**Government before a tournament**: see [tournaments.md](tournaments.md).

## Principles worth quoting

- "The optimal player isn't the one with the biggest damage in one round or the most purposeless companies, but the one who turns
  time, capital and skills into a sustainable cycle." (guide conclusion)
- "Romania wins when information reaches people fast, MUs pull in one direction, the government explains priorities and citizens
  trust that donations and effort are used transparently."
- Tools: verify their methodology (average prices, historic ranges, outdated formulas) against in-game windows and tooltips,
  especially after patches (patriotic bonus, alliance curve, variable deposits).
