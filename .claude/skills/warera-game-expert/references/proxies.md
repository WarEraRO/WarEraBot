# Proxies

A proxy is a country politically controlled by players associated with another community, used for strategic presence,
defense, borders and training new leaders. Romania's proxy list is dynamic: #monitorul-oficial, #strategii-si-scheme or
`/diplomacy`. This whole topic is `[POLICY-RO]` built on game rules marked `[RULE]`.

## Map impact

`[VERSIONED]` From v0.26.0 to v0.27.4 fighters had to travel, so a proxy far from Romania was expensive to defend (every fighter
paid Stamina/oil both ways). Since v0.28 (2026-11-03) distance costs fighters nothing; a distant proxy still risks the −25%
unlinked-capital malus.
Leadership decides per proxy: keep, support locally, or cede.

## Minimum responsibilities

- Log in before the inactivity threshold — internal note: **don't exceed ~70 hours** offline (`ro.proxy_max_offline_hours`) to
  avoid takeover opportunities (live inactivity flag: 3 days).
- Take part in elections and vote to keep institutional control.
- Check the citizen list; report unexpected applications/entries immediately; don't accept until a joint decision (only
  President/VP can manage applications) `[RULE]`.
- Stay active on Discord; coordinate the proxy's agreements with the main country.
- Accept rotation: players can sit in a proxy while on economy and return to fight in Romania.
- Report periodically to Romania's government: citizens, treasury, elections, threats.

## Integrity and treasury

> **Proxy funds are not loot.** Tax and development income belong to the treasury of the country that collected them. Using
> an office to transfer taxes or income to Romania or to individuals can be treated as **abuse of funds** — unusual transfers are
> discussed, documented and, if needed, checked with game moderators.

External aid donated to a proxy can be treated differently from internal taxes, but must stay traceable. Never use trades to
empty a treasury. A proxy must stay functional and legitimate, not formally held by an inactive account. A country with under
10 citizens cannot send or receive money `[RULE]`.

### The admin's Rule 6 ruling `[RULE]` (official article "Clarification Regarding Rule 6", 2026-02-08)

The Romanian policy above matches how moderation enforces **Rule 6** (country funds abuse):
- **A treasury belongs to the country that collected it**, whoever controls it. Funds are handled by that nation's rules; using
  a position of power to move them for personal gain or for another nation is punishable (fines, bans, removal from government or
  military roles). This covers offensive proxies (near enemy borders) and defensive ones.
- **Tax revenue versus external aid**: moving donated or aid money onward is generally allowed; funnelling tax revenue to allies is
  not. **Intent** decides: in Syria, players were put in a 45%-tax company and couldn't quit (a bug), so the treasury emptied to the
  USA and Lithuania was sanctioned. In Lesotho, a 1,500 transfer stood because the treasury held 1,900+ in transfers from
  Portugal, Romania and Venezuela plus citizens' donations.
- **Consult first**: a large, non-standard transfer cleared with moderation beforehand avoids penalties. The ruling cites
  **Bosnia, a Romanian defensive proxy**: Romania asked moderation whether it could reclaim tax lost to the Bosnian treasury, was
  refused (the money came from a legitimate mechanic and Rule 6 protects a proxy's treasury), and complied. That is the model to
  follow.
- Context, now history: the ruling was about the occupation **tax hijack** (occupied regions took up to 40% of tax and development
  income), removed in v0.25.1 (2026-06-14). The principles above don't depend on it. The article also announced tighter money-transfer
  laws and a possible "Loans" feature (not shipped as of the sources).

## Relevant rules `[RULE]`

- Takeover: government **and** congress inactive (threshold disputed 3/4 days); min level 25; cooldown 1 day live vs "5 days per
  user" in guide `[CONFLICT]`.
- Leaving one government and running for president elsewhere: no cooldown `[OBS]` → eases leader rotation.
- Liberation after owner change: 5-day window, then 24 h bilateral non-aggression (blocks instant liberation via proxy).
