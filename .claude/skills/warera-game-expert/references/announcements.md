# In-game announcements and articles

## Country and MU announcements `[RULE][LIVE]`

| Rule | Country announcement | MU announcement |
|---|---|---|
| Who posts | the government (permission *Post announcement*: all government roles) | owner and commanders |
| Who sees it | only the country's citizens | only MU members |
| Rate limit | 2 per hour | 2 per hour |
| Max length | 1,000 characters | 1,000 characters |
| Active | 24 h, then archived | 24 h, then archived |

- The government announcement is also shown at the top of the map.
- Archived announcements stay readable; those with access see view count, can like and comment → a short feedback channel.
- Good for: mobilizations, tournament participation, economic/diplomatic changes, consumable prep, order changes, stable
  administrative info.
- `[OBS]` Long MU announcements push the help buttons down — delete or shorten once it has done its job.

## Articles `[RULE]`

Paste/drag images into articles (up to 10 MB; large images downscaled; animated GIFs stay animated) · text colour picker ·
article categories with icons and colours, including **Giveaway** · GIF picker in chat and articles for Supporter Plan holders.
Live: article creation min level 5, publish cost 3, comment min level 8, tip value 5 (money and gem tips) `[LIVE]`.
Tips `[WIKIPATCH v0.17.7]`: disabled 3 days after publication; max 1 tip per tipper/author pair per 24 h; repeated tipping can be
treated as boosting. Article categories `[WIKI-ONLY]`: News, Guide, Stats, Economy, Politics, Military, Begging, Entertainment, Election,
Other, Official (admins), plus Giveaway (guide). Images: direct links from trusted hosts (Imgur, Giphy, Tenor, Cloudinary, Postimages,
Imgbb, Imagebam) per the wiki; the guide (newer) adds paste/drag upload up to 10 MB. Mention players/countries by pasting their link.

## Discord vs in-game announcements `[POLICY-RO]`

Complementary: in-game announcements carry stable information and reach citizens/members directly; Discord coordinates
real-time action (fast order changes, damage sync, tactics, negotiations, minute-to-minute situations).
